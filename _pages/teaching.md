I am deeply committed to teaching and have experience teaching both large undergraduate courses with more than 100 students and graduate courses with approximately 25 students. I employ an inclusive pedagogy to facilitate lifelong learning, cultivate engaged citizenship, and inspire the next generation of thought-leaders, particularly those from underrepresented areas of the world. As a Teaching Fellow at the Fletcher School of Tufts University in Fall 2025, I delivered lectures that connected rigorous theoretical foundations in the disciplines of international relations and political economy to real-world challenges in energy politics and policy. Through these lectures, I encouraged students to explore the economic, political, and social dimensions of resource governance and climate change issues. Recognizing that learning extends beyond the classroom, I initiated a series of research workshops throughout the semester to mentor students in research design, data analysis, and policy evaluation for their final projects, fostering independent inquiry and practical problem-solving. These workshops encouraged students to engage with contemporary policy debates, critically examine complex global environmental and energy challenges, and evaluate potential solutions. This experience has reinforced my belief that teaching is a form of service: by equipping students with the analytical skills, intellectual curiosity, and practical knowledge to become thoughtful and informed global citizens to address collective challenges facing our world.
